2 and a Possible

Spades, the way the table talks it.

Four players, partners across the table. Short a player or three? Bots fill the empty seats.

The game

Spades is a partnership trick taking game. Spades are trump. You and the player across from you bid how many books (tricks) your team will take, then play 13 tricks (12 with the Kitty on). Follow suit if you can. Highest card of the led suit wins the trick unless a spade cuts it, and the highest spade wins. Spades cannot be led until they are broken, unless your hand is all spades. The trick winner leads the next trick.

Bidding

Each player bids once, starting left of the dealer. Bid a number, bid Nil, or call the signature bid below. Your team bid is your bid plus your partner's bid added together.

Scoring

Make your team bid: 10 points per bid book, plus 1 point per overtrick. Overtricks are sandbags, and every 10 sandbags cost your team 100 points. Miss your bid and your team is set: minus 10 points per bid book. Nil pays plus 100 if the Nil bidder takes no tricks, minus 100 if they take even one. First team to the target score wins: 500 standard, or 250 in a quick game.

The signature bid: 2 and a Possible

Call it when your hand says "I got 2, and maybe one more." It counts as 2 toward your team bid. Take 3 or more books yourself and your team earns a 20 point bonus. Take exactly 2 and you made your bid, no bonus and no penalty. Take 0 or 1 and the guarantee missed: minus 20 points, though that alone does not set your team. The team set is still decided by team books against the team bid.

House Rule: Joker Joker Deuce Deuce

Turn it on and the trump order changes at the top: Big Joker, then Little Joker, then the 2 of Spades, then the 2 of Diamonds, then the Ace of Spades on down. Both jokers go in, the 2 of Hearts and the 2 of Clubs come out, and both deuces count as spades for following suit.

House Rule: The Kitty

Turn it on and each player is dealt 12 cards with 4 cards face down in the kitty. After bidding, the highest individual bidder picks up the kitty (ties go to the earliest bidder), looks at all 16 cards, and puts any 4 back face down. Those 4 are out of the hand and score nothing. Then 12 tricks are played.

Format

V1 plays Partners format: seats 1 and 3 against seats 2 and 4. Bots fill any empty seat when the host starts the game, so one person can play a full table solo.
